Rules

Elden Ring and Witcher 3 both leave the frame to the landscape. Everything here earns its pixels or goes.

  • Mostly empty. No panel behind vitals, tracker or compass. Text sits on the world with a 1px shadow.
  • Gold marks where to look next, or what is chosen. Quest diamond, hotbar slot, boss notch; in menus the focus rule, active tab, chosen toggle option and slider fill. Never text, frames or large fills.
  • Hairlines only. 1px rules and frames. No chamfers, glows, bevels or rounded corners.
  • Sentence case. Title case only for place names like Oakhaven Crossing and boss names; store titles keep their own casing.
  • Only what the store knows. Quests, lore and items come from WorldMillLocalRpgStore. No placeholder flavour text.
  • Legibility over bright sky. Shadow strength follows SunElevationDeg: firmer at noon, nearly gone at dusk.
  • Menus sit on the world. A left gradient scrim, never an opaque box. Title keeps the world live; pause freezes it.
  • No stock chrome. Buttons, toggles and sliders are drawn from hairlines and text; nothing from the default IMGUI skin.
  • Esc steps back one level. Confirm, settings, journal, dialogue, then pause and resume.

In Unity

WorldMill.UI.WorldMillMinimalHud draws all of this in IMGUI with no assets and owns the menu state machine. The player controller pushes vitals through SetVitals, reads IsInputBlocked to ignore gameplay input, and orbits the camera while IsTitleOpen. Settings persist through WorldMillGameSettings.

RegionMethodReads from the store
CompassDrawCompassActiveQuest.targetPosition, camera yaw
ToastDrawToastToastMessage, ToastTimestamp
TrackerDrawTrackerActiveQuest.title, objectives, questCompletionRadius
VitalsDrawVitalsSave.level, xp, xpToNext, equippedWeapon
PromptDrawPromptCanCompleteActiveQuest (the store handles E)
JournalDrawJournalQuests, lore, Save.inventory, SetActiveQuest
TitleDrawTitleHasSaveProgress, Save.level, ActiveQuest, ResetSave
PauseDrawPauseSaveNow; sets Time.timeScale 0 / 1
SettingsDrawSettingsSaveNow, ResetSave; player SetSunElevation, ResetToSpawn, mouseSensitivity, invertY, cameraDistance
